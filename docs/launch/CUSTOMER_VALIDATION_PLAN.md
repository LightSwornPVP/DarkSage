## Purpose

Define a grounded, evidence-driven customer validation plan for the DarkSage expansion.

## Assumptions and Hypotheses

- Hypothesis A: early customers are retail traders who want a private, founder-only companion for strategy validation and trade planning.
- Hypothesis B: value is highest for customers who prioritize trusted local execution, explicit risk guardrails, and trade planning support.
- Hypothesis C: customers will adopt the product when onboarding is simple and the first trade planning experience is clear.
- These assumptions are hypotheses and are subject to validation through research.

## Target Customer Profile and Segments

- Primary segment: experienced retail traders with active accounts, existing broker relationships, and a demand for disciplined paper trading.
- Secondary segment: founder-grade power users who evaluate private dev-mode capabilities and want strict security boundaries.
- Tertiary segment: early beta customers who want a guided transition from paper trading to controlled live pilot access.

## Interview Objectives

- Validate customer pain points around execution confidence, risk management, and broker integration.
- Confirm willingness to pay for controlled trading automation, strategy insights, and supported rollback procedures.
- Identify onboarding friction points for watchlists, stop-loss setup, and paper-trading proposals.
- Test customer understanding of beta limits, private Founder features, and evidence-based safety controls.

## Research Activities and Methods

- Conduct 8–12 in-depth qualitative interviews for primary and secondary segments.
- Run 20–30 short validation surveys across trading communities and existing waitlist prospects.
- Execute 5 usability sessions on the onboarding and paper trading flow.
- Perform pricing-feedback sessions using anchored pricing concepts and willingness-to-pay scenarios.
- Augment qualitative research with usage metrics from early access prototypes and waitlist sign-ups.

## Qualification Criteria and Recruitment

- Recruit participants with active trading experience, broker connectivity interest, and self-reported risk awareness.
- Exclude participants with no prior trading experience or no interest in paper trading or live pilot access.
- Prioritize customers willing to discuss privacy-sensitive execution controls and live-trading safety.
- Use recruitment channels: existing DarkSage waitlist, trading forums, direct founder outreach, and referral networks.

## Interview Question Categories

- Problem validation: current workflow, pain points, and trust gaps in trading tools.
- Experience validation: onboarding expectations, feature discoverability, and desired automation controls.
- Safety validation: risk-profile comfort, order reconciliation expectations, and incident response preferences.
- Pricing validation: value of paper trading, live pilot access, and premium feature bundles.

## Success Criteria and Decision Rules

- At least 70% of qualified interviewees agree the product addresses a high-value need.
- At least 50% of target users express willingness to pay for controlled paper trading or live pilot features.
- Onboarding usability tests show task completion for paper-trading setup with fewer than two major friction points per session.
- Clear negative signals in safety language, risk transparency, or pricing lead to scope adjustments before customer beta.

## Beta Qualification and Waitlist Flows

- Define beta qualification criteria based on trading experience, device trust, and willingness to participate in pilots.
- Maintain a waitlist segment for Founder Beta candidates and a separate customer beta segment.
- Use consent language that explains data access, privacy safeguards, and the difference between founder-only and customer-facing builds.

## Evidence and Schedule

- Record interview summaries, quote themes, and validation scores in `docs/launch/CUSTOMER_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` and supporting research notes.
- Owner: DS-022.
- Initial research window: 4 weeks from foundation pass.
- Review findings weekly and update hypotheses only when evidence supports the change.
- Decision rule: if evidence is inconclusive after the first 12 interviews, expand sample size by 8 and re-evaluate.
