## Purpose

This document defines the overall launch readiness package, including the launch gate matrix, required artifacts, operational readiness, rollback readiness, and post-launch review plan.

## Scope

This checklist covers Founder Alpha, Founder Beta, Customer Beta, Live-Trading Pilot, Commercial Release, Launch-Day, and Post-Launch Stabilization Exit readiness.

## Required Deliverables

- Defined launch gate matrix with explicit entry criteria, blocking checks, evidence requirements, and approver roles.
- A customer validation plan for early access, willingness-to-pay, onboarding, and beta qualification.
- A legal and policy artifact inventory with required review stages.
- A billing test scenario matrix covering payment and entitlement edge cases.
- A transactional email register covering customer-facing notifications.
- A product analytics event register for key customer journeys and activation signals.
- Operational runbooks for launch day, rollback, and the first 90 days of live operation.
- Support operations and incident response plans.

## Readiness Principles

- No gate passes solely on narrative claims.
- Blocking checks must have evidence and approvals recorded.
- Launch decisions must be traceable and reversible.
- Customer-facing readiness must remain distinct from Founder-private research and local tooling.
