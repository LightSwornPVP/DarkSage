## Purpose

Define the checkpoint plan for the first 90 days after launch.

## Checkpoints

- First hour: verify release stability, alerting, and rollback readiness.
- First 24 hours: monitor incidents, onboarding completion, and activation.
- First 7 days: validate conversion, retention, and support load.
- First 30 days: review trial conversion, churn, and feature adoption.
- Days 31–60: assess trends, cost, and operational load.
- Days 61–90: validate product-market fit adjustments and roadmap updates.

## Metrics

Track errors, incidents, support volume, onboarding completion, activation, time-to-first-value, trial conversion, paid conversion, retention, churn, feature adoption, false alerts, rejected-trade explanations, broker failures, order reconciliation, risk-rule interventions, service cost, and customer satisfaction.
