## Purpose

Capture the readiness criteria for Founder and customer beta programs.

## Founder Beta Readiness

- Founder Beta is a distinct stage after Founder Alpha and before Customer Beta.
- Founder-only build and private configuration are separated from customer-facing beta builds.
- Customer builds must never include private models, private prompts, private tools, private repositories, or private credentials used by Founder Beta.
- Launch gate definitions for Founder Alpha, Founder Beta, and Customer Beta are documented in `LAUNCH_GATE_MATRIX.csv`.
- No automatic production deployment or broker execution authority is introduced during Founder Beta readiness.

## Customer Beta Readiness

- Beta recruitment and qualification criteria are defined.
- Customer validation plan includes ideal customer profile, willingness-to-pay testing, and onboarding usability testing.
- Waitlist, early access, and privacy notice flows are defined.
- Analytics events and email notifications are mapped to beta milestones.
